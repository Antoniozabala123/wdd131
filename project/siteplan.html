1. Site Name

SpeakUp Academy

Reason for choice: 'Speak Up' is an English phrasal verb that means to express oneself clearly and without fear. This name perfectly represents the main objective of the portal: helping Spanish speakers overcome the fear of speaking and develop fluency and confidence from day one.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this educational platform is to provide an interactive and accessible environment for learning conversational English. The site will offer essential vocabulary guides for real-world scenarios (travel, work, interviews), dynamic pronunciation practices, and interactive self-evaluation quizzes.
